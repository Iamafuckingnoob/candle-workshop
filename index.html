Why Now

This is a working practice. Not a personality trait.

People talk about candle magick like it's an aesthetic. Light a candle, set an intention, post it. That isn't the work.

The work is knowing why you're using a green candle on a Friday at this hour, what herbs lock the spell, what oil opens the road. The work is reading the flame after you've spoken to it. The work is knowing when a working has held and when it hasn't.

I've been practicing this every day for years. Before I taught it to anyone, I lived it. This workshop is what I would have wanted when I started.

No bypassing. No fluff. No rebranded astrology. Real candle magick the way it's actually done, taught by someone who actually does it.

The Two Live Sessions

One day to learn. One day to work.

Day One is the full teaching. Every correspondence, every mechanic, the whole architecture of candle magick. Day Two is the practical. We cast together, live, with your kit in your hands.

i
Day One · The Theory

The Full Architecture of Candle Magick.

The five elements as the spine of every spell. Candle colors and what they actually move. Herbs as binding agents, oils as accelerants. Which ones, when, and why. Lunar timing, planetary days, and ritual structure from opening to closing. Anointing direction, dressing technique, petition writing as command not wish. By the end of Day One you understand exactly what a working is and how it holds. This is the entire grimoire transmitted live.

ii
Day Two · The Practical

We Cast a Working Together.

You light your candle, I light mine. We move through cleansing, dressing, petition, and the spoken command. Together, live. You learn flame reading on your own flame, in real time. We work the disposal and the closing properly. After: how to read what unfolds in the days that follow, and what to do if the working stalls. You don't leave with notes. You leave with a working already in motion.

Who Teaches This

Practiced. Not theorized.

I'm Deeksha. I've been practicing as a witch and priestess for years before I ever taught a single class. My altar isn't decorative. The spells in this grimoire are workings I've cast in my own life.

I work daily with five deities: Shiva, Shakti, Hekate, Isis, and Circe. My framework is syncretic but it isn't borrowed. Every correspondence in this course has been tested in my own practice before it landed in the curriculum.

I founded The Moon Goddess (intention oils, candles, ritual blends) and Trigandha (bambooless incense). I run them from my own altar room. The work is the brand. The brand is the work.

I don't dilute. I teach magick as a real discipline, not a vibe. If you want comfort and crystals, this isn't for you. If you want to actually learn the craft and practice it, you're in the right place.
